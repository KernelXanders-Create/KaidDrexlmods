| The Somme | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme) | Trench belts in depth (front, support, reserve) linked by communication trenches. Belts are the op structure. | A |

| Everglades | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everglades) | "River of grass": sheet flow over limestone, sawgrass sloughs about 0.3-0.6 m deep, long open sight lines. | B |

## Sea-level rule (04)
All three swamps sit on land from about +0.2 to +3 m, with water cut below 0 m. In Arma any terrain below 0 m is "sea", so every pool, slough and river is connected sea water at 0 m. **No open coast is shown:** the map edges rise (moraines, ridges, bluffs), so the swamp reads as inland. That also keeps us away from Blackfen's coast-delta look.
