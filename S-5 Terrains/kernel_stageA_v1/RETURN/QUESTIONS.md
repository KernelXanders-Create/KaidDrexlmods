# Questions for Miran and command (Stage A, PROPOSED)

Each question changes the design. I've given my recommended default, and I carried on with that default until you say otherwise. Format: Option A / B / C, pick one.

## Both maps
**Q1. Which concept per map?**
- Islands: A Pillar Reach / B Ember Chain / C The Shallows. **Default: A.**
- Swamp: A The Mire Line / B The Glow / C River Country. **Default: A.**

See SUMMARY.md for the reasons.

**Q2. Can droids ever cross water?**
- A: never. They hold their own island or firm patch and counterattack over land only.
- B: only in Zeus-driven boats.
- C: AI boats and wading allowed, after tests W1 and W2 pass.

**Default: A** (04 rates amphibious AI as a big risk).

**Q3. How many ops?**
- A: 6 ops (3 weekends).
- B: 4 ops (2 weekends).

**Default: A.** Every concept is laid out for 6. If you pick B, I'd drop the side ops (islands A: ops 3 and 4 merge; B: op 4; C: op 4; swamp A: op 3; B: op 2 or 3; C: op 4) and keep the opening and the finale.

**Q4. What boats, landing craft and hovers do we actually have in the modset?** This is a fact I can't see from mod names. It decides whether vehicles reach islands and whether the swamp's hovers work on water.
- A: we have AI-drivable boats and landing craft.
- B: player-crewed only.
- C: none, so air only (LAAT sling).

**Default: B** (player and Zeus boats, light vehicles by sling).

**Q5. Does our sea level move?** Arma may change the water height with tides or waves; I haven't checked on our modset. Swamps at +0.2 m and reef flats at -0.5 m are sensitive to that.
- A: run test W3 before Stage B.
- B: assume it's fixed.

**Default: A** (it's a cheap test and protects every concept).

## Islands
**Q6. Concept A only: Market Isle and Gun Tower Isle nearly touch on the sketch** (a wadeable shelf, about 330 m at -0.3 m, MEASURED on the sketch).
- A: keep it as a wading route.
- B: deepen it so they're separate islands.

**Default: B**, so each island stays a distinct place.

**Q7. Setting.**
- A: original worlds (A tropical, B volcanic).
- B: canon names (Scarif, Ahch-To, Mon Cala).

**Default:** originals for A and B (canon-era and lore clashes); Mon Cala for C (a canon CIS invasion fits well).

**Q8. Op 1 start.**
- A: the fleet offshore, with LAATs and boats from out at sea.
- B: a pre-held Republic islet on the map.

**Default: A** for the beach-landing feel. Note that we don't give LZs (house rule); platoons choose.

## Swamp
**Q9. Is a trench war right for 2nd Company?** Swamp A is literally trench belts.
- A: yes.
- B: lighter trenches, more open bog.
- C: no; pick swamp B or C instead.

**Default: A.**

**Q10. Swamp B needs 2 new comps (the Titan and the citadel lab).** That is at the 1-2 limit.
- A: OK.
- B: swap the Titan for a reused facility.

**Default: A**, only if you pick B.

**Q11. Swamp C has one vehicle bridge over the river.**
- A: keep one (a strong objective).
- B: add a second bridge or pontoon in op 5.

**Default: B.**

**Q12. Light and colour direction.** These are proposed palettes:

Pick per concept or veto. **Default:** as proposed. All differ from Blackfen's violet.
