## Real-world teachers (what each one teaches us)
| Place | Link | What it teaches | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Palau Rock Islands | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_Islands) | Hundreds of small limestone islets, undercut at the waterline into "mushrooms", and sheltered lagoons between them. Lots of small islets break sight lines. The undercut has to be rock objects, because a heightmap can't overhang. | A (Mushroom Field) |
| Phang Nga Bay | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phang_Nga_Bay) | Tall karst towers with "hongs" (roofless collapsed-cave lagoons inside an island, reached through a notch). This is the model for a hidden basin inside an island. | A (Twin Hongs, the Crown's bowl) |
| Ha Long Bay | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%E1%BA%A1_Long_Bay) | Fields of towers at many scales. Floating fishing villages tucked in coves. Haze softens far towers, which matters for the 2-6 km distant read. | A (north karst field, stilt towns) |
| Deception Island | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deception_Island) | A flooded caldera that ships enter through one narrow breach in the rim. This is the model for "the facility is inside a volcano and you sail in". | B (the Caldera) |
| Azores (Sete Cidades, Faial) | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sete_Cidades_Massif) | Caldera rims, crater lakes, green pasture on steep volcanic slopes, cliffy coasts with small black beaches. | B |
| Lanzarote (Timanfaya) | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timanfaya_National_Park) | Lava flows and cinder cones. Lava fingers running into the sea make natural beach "ribs", which give cover on a black beach. | B (Black Strand, Cinder Cone) |
| Faroe Islands | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faroe_Islands) | High sea cliffs, narrow sounds between islands, sea stacks ([Drangarnir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drangarnir)), villages at the heads of sounds. | B (Fjord Isle, Stack Garden) |
| Maldives / atolls | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atoll) | A ring reef with wadeable reef flats, sand motus on the ring, deep passes and a lagoon. Every depth band in one place. | C |
| Makatea (French Polynesia) | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makatea) | An uplifted coral island: a cliff rim all round and a mined plateau inside. It looks like a low wall from the sea. | C (Makatea) |
| Nauru phosphate pinnacles | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nauru) | Strip-mining left a field of coral limestone pinnacles: a natural maze. | C (Pinnacle Maze) |
| Raja Ampat | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raja_Ampat_Islands) | Jungle down to the waterline, stilt villages, lagoons with very clear water. Good for colour. | A, C colour |

## Coast landform rules we followed (from the house rules)
- **Hard rock** (karst, basalt) makes headlands, towers and stacks with a narrow rock shelf at the foot.
- **Sediment** (sand, tephra, coral rubble) makes beaches, spits, sandbars and motus.
- **Reef** makes wide flats (about -0.3 to -1.2 m) and deep passes.
- **People** make quays, moles, causeways and dams.
- Every shore in the sketches gets a shelf, and its width varies along the coast (MEASURED in code: 0.35-2.2x the base width). Nothing drops straight to -10 m.
