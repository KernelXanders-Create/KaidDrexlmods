# Questions and change requests for Kernel, ops 3 to 8 (12 Nov), most important first

These cover all six plans. Items from our 11 Nov note (ops 3-5) are folded in where ops 6-8 repeat them, and marked "(11 Nov)".

1. **Road grades: the same fault is in ops 6, 7 and 8.** Your profiles rise about 0.63 m per point. On curves and switchbacks your points are 0.4-3 m apart, so the real pitch there is far over 14 %. We measured along the points you send.
   - **Op 6:** 12 of 13 roads are over 14 % on a 20 m base. The worst is the climb from the op 4 net: 20.7 % over 20 m and 25.9 % over 10 m. About 1.4 km of 13.1 km is over 14 %.
   - **Op 7:** 5 of 15 roads. The worst is the crest road to the Crew Barracks: 19.2 % over 20 m and 22.2 % over 10 m.
   - **Op 8:** 4 of 13 roads. The worst is the 4.3 km road to the Yard Power Plant: 20.9 % over 20 m and 24.3 % over 10 m. The other three are just over (14.3 %).
   - (11 Nov) Ops 3, 4 and 5 had 7 of 11, 7 of 17 and 7 of 10 roads over.
   - Please re-profile so that every stretch of 10 m or more holds 14 %. Measure on the points you hand over, not on the nominal 4.5 m spacing.

2. **Ops 4, 6, 7 and 8 do not reach the airfield.** Every op 4-8 site gives "vehicles on the AI road net (Airfield, causeways, Cinder, Ember Crescent)" as an approach.
   - On the height grid, the ops 6, 7 and 8 roads form one net with op 4's Causeway Foot roads, and they are joined at matching levels.
   - That net is about 635 m from op 4's trunk, at the north causeway's cone end. The trunk is about 638 m from the airfield's roads, at the south causeway head (11 Nov).
   - Please draw both links inside the rules, or say exactly which ramp pieces they need and where.
   - Op 4's trunk cannot be a 20 m step: the measured gap is 638 m.

3. **Op 8: the final is not on the vehicle net.** The Yard's inner-shore net is separate.
   - We measure about 630 m from the nearest inner-shore road end to the nearest arm road. Your lowest step is 19.4 m over 355 m.
   - The Yard is the end of the run, so its vehicle way in should be drawn, not left as a gap.
   - Please draw the ramp road from the south arm down to the inner shore, with its points, levels and band.
   - If it truly cannot hold 14 % and 4 m, please say what piece it needs (length, rise, width).
