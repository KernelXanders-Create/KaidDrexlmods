## Briefing
**Situation.**
- **Ground.** Black Strand is a low shield volcano with black beaches, in the south-west corner of the islands. The Stack Garden, a reef of sea stacks, lies just east of it, a short wade across the reef flat.
- **Enemy.** The CIS hold the whole island.
  - A bunker line and the Lava Rib gun pits cover the south beach. Pylons fed by the geothermal plant shield the beach guns.
  - Flak sits on the summit crater. A sensor stack offshore cues it onto gunships.
  - Perys, the harbour village, is their depot. A fort under the sea arch holds the Stack Garden water.
  - **Smaller positions** dig in around the main sites, 115-381 m out:
    - heavy guns, some with AA: two behind the bunker line, one each above the gun pits, the turret bunkers, the West Cove landers and the North Strand AA;
    - an AA platform just below the summit flak;
    - a comms array over the fuel farm;
    - checkpoints on the road into Perys and on the crater road.
- **Friendly.** The Acclamator holds offshore to the south. Nothing on the islands is ours yet.

**Mission.** Take Perys and give the Republic its first FOB on this world.

**Command chooses.** The op is won with **Perys** plus **any two** of these four:
- **Strand Bunker Line** (platoon): its guns cover the whole black beach. Taking it opens the south beach.
- **Geothermal Plant** (platoon, destructible): it powers the shield pylons. Blow it and the pylons' shields drop. It stands about 900 m from Perys, the closest of the four.
- **Lava Rib Gun Pits** (two-squad): guns in the lava ribs enfilade the south beach. Silencing them opens its west half.
- **Summit Crater Post** (two-squad): flak and observation on the summit. Taking it opens the island's sky to LAATs.

Supporting sites pay off if you take them:
- the **Offshore Sensor Stack** (air only): kill it and the first landings arrive unannounced;
- the **Droid Artillery Park** (air prep: garrisoned from the start for pilots to soften);
- the **Shield Pylon Ridge** (destructible): blow the pylons and the beach guns lose their shields;
- the **Ash Field Depot**: it burns, and its logs map the gun line;
- the **Black Sand Fuel Farm**: it burns, and the landers stop coming;
- the **West Cove Landers**: a second landing, on the west cove;
- the **Lighthouse Cape**: it blinds the Stack Garden guns to the east beach;
- the **Mortar Terraces**: they stop the mortars on the Perys approach;
- the **Crater Road Checkpoint**: it opens the road from Perys to the summit;
- the **Vent Field Radio**: the first intel on the Yard;
- the **North Strand AA**: it opens the air route north;
- the **Rib Turret Bunkers**: a covered way to the gun pits;
- the **Arch Islet Fort** and **Reef Pillboxes** on the Stack Garden: they make the reef wade safe.

**Getting there.**
- **You spawn on the Acclamator. Your Zeuses get you ashore.** There is no boat or transport system to call.
- **No landing zones are given.** Where you land is command's call.
- The ship is about 2.5 km off the Strand Bunker Line and about 5 km from Perys (MEASURED by you, straight line).
- **No order is set.** Beach first, summit first, or straight at Perys all work.

**After the op.** The FOB is in Perys. If Perys is not taken, op 2 starts from the fleet again. (Op 2 starts from the ship either way: your 10 Nov ruling.)
