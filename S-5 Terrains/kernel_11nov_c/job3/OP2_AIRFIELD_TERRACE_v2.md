## Briefing
**Situation.**
- **Ground.** The Airfield Terrace is a raised marine terrace in the south-east, with a flat top at about 45 m and cliffs to the sea. Wreck Cove cuts in under its south-west cliffs. The causeway north to Mole Rock starts at its north end.
- **Enemy.** The CIS Super Airbase on the terrace flies every droid fighter over these islands.
  - AA bases cover both ends of the terrace, west and east.
  - The fuel farm, the munitions store and the power bank feed the airbase.
  - A freighter lies aground in Wreck Cove.
  - **Two air-defence forts** stand on rock outcrops, one about 220 m from the West Terrace AA and one about 400 m from the East Terrace AA. Each is reached on foot only by a ramp to its gate.
  - **Two star bastions and a gun keep** guard the approaches. One bastion is about 400 m from the airbase and one about 300 m above the cliff stair; the gun keep is about 260 m from the fuel farm.
  - **Gun, AA and checkpoint positions** dig in around the cove, the cliffs, the north beach, the causeway head, the depot and the east ridge, 120-400 m from the sites they guard.
- **Friendly.** The Acclamator is anchored south-west of the Terrace. Black Strand is ours if op 1 was won (see History).

**Mission.** Take the Terrace Airbase. It becomes the Republic's FARP, and the CIS fighters are gone from the caldera sky.

**Command chooses.** The op is won with the **Terrace Airbase** plus **any two** of these four:
- **Terrace Fuel Farm** (platoon, destructible): the fighters stay grounded without it. It makes a big burn.
- **Control Tower** (two-squad): flight control. Its logs show the Yard's frigate schedule (intel).
- **West Terrace AA** (two-squad): covers the western air approach. Taking it lets LAATs put troops on the terrace.
- **East Terrace AA** (two-squad): covers the causeway and the eastern approach. Taking it opens the causeway to air cover.

Supporting sites pay off if you take them:
- the **Parked Droid Fighters** (air prep: garrisoned from the start for pilots to soften);
- the **Wreck Cove** freighter (its manifest points at the Yard) and the **Cliff Stair Post** above it (the cove becomes a way up);
- the **South Cliff Guns**: silence them and the south cliffs open to landings;
- the **Workers' Camp** and the **West Terrace Depot**: covered start lines onto the runway's north side and west end;
- the **Revetment Bunkers** (they open the runway's east half) and the **East Ridge Trench** (the runway's east end and the radar road);
- the **Radar Point** (destructible): it blinds the airbase's early warning;
- the **Terrace Power Bank** (destructible): it slows the East Terrace AA;
- the **Munitions Store** (destructible): a big secondary blast;
- the **North Beach Landing**: it opens the north beach;
