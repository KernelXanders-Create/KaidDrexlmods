# Masks: what each layer is (drop 2, 24 Nov 2026)

`suddra_masks.npz`: every layer is 4096 x 4096, same grid as `suddra_height_cm.npz` (4 m cells, row 0 = south, `[i, j]` at x = 4j, y = 4i, metres, SW origin). Stored as uint8 (0/1 unless noted). Shares are shares of all 16.78 million cells. Every number below is MEASURED on the arrays in this pack unless it is tagged ASSUMED. Meanings are taken from our terrain notes where they exist, and from the data otherwise; anything inferred is tagged ASSUMED.

| Layer | Values | What it is | Measured facts | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `water` | 0 / 1 | The designed water bodies: reed-sea, meres, kettle lakes, the Drain, boat channels. | 27.33 % of the map, 73.4 km2. 99.68 % of cells below 0 m are water. 0.155 % of the map is water with ground at or above 0 m (wade cells reach +0.79 m). | MEASURED |
| `depth_band` | 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 | Band of each water cell. 0 = not water. 1 = wade (reed-covered shallows). 2 = deep (isolated meres). 3 = boat (channels and lakes joined to the Drain; designed 1.5 m deep or more). | Band 1: 36.6 km2, ground -0.91 to +0.79 m, mean -0.49. Band 2: 21.4 km2, mean -1.85 m. Band 3: 15.4 km2, mean -2.04 m. Names 1 = wade, 3 = boat: from our notes and the heights (every `boat_core` cell lies in band 3). | Counts MEASURED; names for 2 vs 3 ASSUMED |
| `sea` | 0 / 1 | Engine sea: the cells below 0 m (Arma treats all ground under 0 as sea). | 27.18 % of the map; equals "height below 0" on 99.68 % of cells (the rest is rounding at the shoreline). Not open sea: on Suddra it is the swamp's own water. | MEASURED (equality); meaning ASSUMED |
| `ai_dry` | 0 / 1 | Ground the AI can stand and walk on (the dry network and everything joined to it). | 44.87 % of the map. The design rule was "at least +0.5 m"; 99.66 % of cells at or above +0.5 m are in it, but a few cells are lower (lowest -1.92 m; first percentile +0.54 m). 1,921 cells are both `ai_dry` and `water`. Treat it as a network mask, not a pure height test. | MEASURED; "AI walks" ASSUMED from the build notes |
| `net_core` | 0 / 1 | The core line of the AI walking network: levees, eskers, ridges, the rail, bunds and the site islands that join it. | 0.60 % of the map; every cell is also `ai_dry`; lowest ground +0.53 m. | MEASURED; content ASSUMED from the notes |
| `boat_core` | 0 / 1 | The core of the boat network: cells a boat can use (the channels and lakes joined to the Drain). | 5.35 % of the map; every cell is `water` and in `depth_band` 3. The boat network is split at the Locks (the dam). | MEASURED; meaning ASSUMED |
| `islands` | 0 / 1 | The listed firm islands inside water bodies (the only dry ground the design allows inside a water body). | 0.16 % of the map; mean ground +0.22 m. All island cells lie inside the `water` mask (the mask covers the whole body, islands included) and only 7 % are `ai_dry`; it is a marker of the island ground, not a list of walkable cells. | MEASURED; meaning ASSUMED |
| `designed_straights` | 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 | Straight features that exist on purpose (our rule: every straight feature is listed with its reason). 2 = the rail embankment; 3 = the Drain Locks dam crest; 1 = the short raised banks (bunds) joining two islands. | Value 1: 1,601 cells (mean +1.25 m). Value 2: 28,364 cells (mean +4.36 m), identical to the `rail` layer. Value 3: 381 cells (mean +1.30 m); the Locks crossing line meets it. Our straights list holds 49 rail pieces, 22 bunds and 1 dam crest. | 2 and 3 MEASURED; 1 ASSUMED by elimination |
| `rail` | 0 / 1 | The rail embankment (a raised straight bed running south-west to north across the map; raised ground and a map line, built as an embankment). | 0.17 % of the map; mean ground +4.36 m; identical to `designed_straights == 2`. | MEASURED |
| `crater` | 0 / 1 | Crater bowls and rims (our terrain build made 912 craters, 766 with ponds). | 0.87 % of the map; ground -1.79 to +7.90 m; 43 % of crater cells are water. The footprint (bowl only, or bowl plus rim) is not stated in our notes. | MEASURED; footprint ASSUMED |
| `zone` | 0-5 | The landform zone: 0 = outside the swamp domain (high ground at the map edge and the north moraine); 1 = reed-sea matrix (Z1); 2 = Drain, kettle lakes, thaw meres, bog pools, boat channels (Z2); 3 and 4 = patch zones (Z3 and Z4, see below); 5 = sand ridges and esker belts (Z5). | Shares of the swamp domain (zones 1-5): Z1 49.7 %, Z2 18.7 %, Z3 10.6 %, Z4 10.2 %, Z5 10.8 %; zone 0 is 27.9 % of the map (mean ground +12.1 m). Water share per zone: 0 = 0.9 %, 1 = 12.2 %, 2 = 100 %, 3 = 99.9 %, 4 = 22.0 %, 5 = 0 %. | MEASURED; the names of Z3 (mostly water, so shallow meres) and Z4 (island and bund patches) ASSUMED |

## Not in this file
`water_id`, `padmask`, `tex_w`, `fens`, `rift`, `hard`, `drainage`, `cliff` (internal or empty layers) are left out. `rift`, `hard`, `drainage` and `cliff` are all zero on this build.

## Reading tips (ASSUMED, from how the layers overlap)
- Dry ground to place on: the `water` mask alone is not enough (island cells sit inside it); use the heights (at or above +0.5 m, as our floors do) and `ai_dry` together.
- Boats: `boat_core`. Walking: `ai_dry` and `net_core`. Both are for planning; our own checks are re-run on every seat we build.
