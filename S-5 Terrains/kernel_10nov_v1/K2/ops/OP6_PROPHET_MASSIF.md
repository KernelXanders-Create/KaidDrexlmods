# Op 6: Prophet Massif and Signal Isle (week 3), PROPOSED briefing

† marks something I invented that is not in the purpose map.

## Briefing
**Situation.**
- **Ground.** The Prophet Massif is the pale limestone peak at the east of Ember Crescent, about 420 m and the highest on the map.
- **Enemy.**
  - An old monastery on its summit holds the CIS sensor crown, which watches the whole east and directs the turbolaser battery on Signal Isle.
  - That battery keeps the Republic fleet off the eastern sea.
  - Ferry pens on the massif's north cape run troops and shells across the 2.4 km channel to the battery.
  - Below the massif, a bunker line covers the black Kamar Strand, and Kamar Village billets its garrison.
- **Friendly.** We hold Causeway Foot. Vehicles reach Ember Crescent by the causeways.

**Mission.** Take the Prophet Monastery and its sensor crown. Silence the Signal Isle battery so the fleet can close.

**Command chooses.** The op is won with the **Prophet Monastery** plus **any two** of:
- **Signal Isle Turbolaser Battery** (platoon): the fleet's eastern sea opens. Boat or air only.
- **Kamar Strand Bunker Line** (two-squad): opens the beach to boats and supply.
- **North Point Ferry Pens** (two-squad): cuts the battery's resupply and gives our boats the short hop.
- **Signal Ridge Comms** (two-squad): leaves the battery firing blind. On Signal Isle.

Supporting sites pay off if you take them:
- the Prophet Road Checkpoint opens the vehicle way up;
- the Upper Spring Outpost and the Limestone Quarry give covered climbs to the summit;
- the Massif AA opens the air route to Signal Isle;
- Kamar Village's quay is a boat landing for the Signal Isle run.

The East Slope Gun Line is populated from the start for air prep. The deep-sea sensor stack can only be hit from the air.

**Getting there.**
- **On foot and by vehicle** up the crescent's outer east slope from Causeway Foot (3.3 km to the summit, all on land; MEASURED).
- **By boat** to Kamar Strand.
- **Signal Isle only by boat or LAAT.**
- **No landing zones are given. No order is set.** Summit first or battery first both work.

**After the op.** The FOB moves to the monastery. The crest road to the Rim Chora (op 7) opens from the east.

## Zeus brief (5 lines)
1. **Held:** the monastery and sensor crown, the quarry, the Kamar beach defences, the ferry pens and the AA on the massif; on Signal Isle, the battery, its depot, comms, chapel spotter and AA. 19 sites, planned garrison about 955 if all filled.
2. **Reserve:** on the massif from the summit side, west of the monastery. On Signal Isle only its own garrison, from the battery's inland side. Spawn 250-400 m out, unseen.
3. **No droid crosses the Signal channel.** The ferry pens are targets, not a live ferry.
4. **The turbolaser battery fires only at the fleet's anchorage (story), never at players.** No scripted artillery.
5. **Must not happen:** AI convoys on the monastery road only after its lane is cleared; no LZ marked on the summit.
