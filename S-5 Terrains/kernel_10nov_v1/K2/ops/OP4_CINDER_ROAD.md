# Op 4: Cinder Road (week 2), PROPOSED briefing

† marks something I invented that is not in the purpose map.

## Briefing
**Situation.**
- **Ground.** Two causeways are the only road onto Ember Crescent, the big rim of the caldera.
  - The south causeway runs from the Airfield Terrace over Mole Rock to the Cinder Cone.
  - The north causeway runs from the cone to Causeway Foot on the crescent.
- **Enemy.** The CIS fort in the cone's crater is the gate of that road. Guns on the cone's east flank cover both causeways. A gun checkpoint on Mole Rock closes the bend, starting closed. An AA platform on the East Skerries shoots LAATs on the eastern run into the caldera.
- **Friendly.** We hold the Airfield Terrace and its FARP.

**Mission.** Take the Cinder Cone Fort and open both causeways to Republic vehicles. Take Causeway Foot as the next FOB.

**Command chooses.** The op is won with the **Cinder Cone Fort** plus **any two** of:
- **Mole Rock Gate** (two-squad): lets vehicles reach the cone.
- **Cone Flank Batteries** (platoon): silence the guns on both causeways. They are populated from the start for air prep.
- **Causeway Foot** (platoon): the town at the landfall and the next FOB.
- **Skerry AA Platform** (two-squad): opens the eastern air route. Only boats or air reach it.

Supporting sites pay off if you take them:
- the South Foot Depot gives the fort's gate code (intel);
- the Steam Vent Bores darken the fort's radars;
- the Black Beach Landers open the cone's north beach;
- the Cone Quarry stops the mortar crews on the south causeway;
- the South Arm Road Checkpoint opens op 8's southern road.

**Getting there.**
- **Armour and infantry** go up the south causeway from the FARP (0.9 km to Mole Rock, then 1.1 km to the cone; MEASURED).
- **Boats** reach the cone's flanks and the Skerries (the Skerry channel is about 0.5 km).
- **No landing zones are given.**
- **No order is set.** Push the road, land on the cone's beaches, or hit Causeway Foot first and take the fort from both ends.

**After the op.** The FOB moves to Causeway Foot; if it is not taken, the FOB goes in the cone fort and op 6 starts over the north causeway. Vehicles reach Ember Crescent for the rest of the run.

## Zeus brief (5 lines)
1. **Held:** the cone fort, the flank batteries, Mole Rock Gate, Causeway Foot, the Skerry AA and the causeway guns. 17 sites, planned garrison about 855 if all filled.
2. **Reserve:** on the cone from the South Foot Depot side; on the crescent from behind Causeway Foot (west). Spawn 250-400 m out, unseen. The Skerries hold only their own garrison.
3. **AI convoys** may use the causeways (the AI road net). Clear the lanes first. No convoy crosses the open water.
4. **The flank batteries and quarry mortars are targets only.** No scripted fire on players.
5. **Must not happen:** Mole Rock Gate does not open by itself; no droid boats; armour is never pushed off the road net onto beaches it can't climb.
