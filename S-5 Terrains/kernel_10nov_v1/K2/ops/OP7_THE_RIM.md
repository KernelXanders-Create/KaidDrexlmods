# Op 7: The Rim (week 4), PROPOSED briefing

† marks something I invented that is not in the purpose map.

## Briefing
**Situation.**
- **Ground.** The Yard lies inside the caldera, 1.2 km below Ember Crescent's crest.
- **Enemy.**
  - The **Rim Chora** is the Yard's crew town on the clifftop, with a CIS bastion in its kastro. The Skala path runs from it down to the Yard.
  - The **Skaros Rock Keep** juts into the lagoon, and its guns cover the Yard's water.
  - On the north rim, **Oyra** garrisons the **North Narrows**, the boat gap into the lagoon. Two batteries close it: one on the crescent and one on Ash Crescent's north tip.
  - **Oyra Head** carries a radar, a fortified lander and a windmill row that powers the Narrows guns.
- **Friendly.** Depending on what ran before, we hold some or all of the Prophet Monastery, Kastro Quay and Sound Head.

**Mission.** Take the Rim Chora and the north rim. Open the Skala path to the Yard. Open the North Narrows to boats.

**Command chooses.** The op is won with the **Rim Chora** plus **any two** of:
- **Skaros Rock Keep** (platoon): silences the guns on the Yard's water.
- **Oyra** (platoon): the north rim town and the Narrows garrison.
- **Narrows East Battery** (two-squad): populated from the start for air prep. The Narrows open to boats only when both batteries fall.
- **Chora AA Umbrella** (two-squad): opens the rim to LAATs.

Supporting sites pay off if you take them:
- the Skala Path Head opens op 8's infantry way down;
- the Crew Barracks give the Yard's shift roster (intel);
- the Chora Power Bank drops the kastro's shield;
- the Oyra Head Radar hides boats on the Coral Shelf;
- the Narrows West Battery on Ash's north tip is the Narrows' other jaw, reached by boat.

The north skerry sensor can only be hit from the air.

**Getting there.** Any of three ways, and command picks:
1. Along the crest road from the Prophet Monastery (op 6).
2. By boat from Kastro Quay through the Coral Shelf to Oyra Head (op 5).
3. By boat from Sound Head to Ash Crescent's north tip (op 3).

No landing zones are given. No order is set.

**After the op.** The Chora is held. The Skala path down to the Yard is open. The Narrows are open to boats if both batteries fell.

## Zeus brief (5 lines)
1. **Held:** the Chora (town and bastion), Skaros Keep, Oyra, the Narrows batteries, Oyra Head, the rim AA, power and barracks. 18 sites, planned garrison about 880 if all filled.
2. **Reserve:** for the Chora, from the crew barracks side; for Oyra, from the north rim trench; on Ash's north tip, only its own garrison. Spawn 250-400 m out, unseen. Never send a reserve up the caldera cliff.
3. **No droid crosses the Narrows or the lagoon.**
4. **The Narrows batteries and Skaros guns are targets only.** No scripted fire on players.
5. **Must not happen:** the Skala path is not blocked by script after its head falls; Yard units do not climb up into the Chora (the Yard is op 8).

## History (pick by the results before; op 7 can follow op 3, 5 or 6)
- **If op 6 was won (the monastery is ours):** "The sensor crown is dark, and the fleet holds the eastern sea. The fleet's Venators† can now close on the caldera."
- **If op 5 was won (Kastro Quay is the FOB):** "Boats from Kastro Quay can thread the Coral Shelf to Oyra Head. The Narrows guns are all that stand between them and the lagoon."
- **If op 5 left the Lantern Rock Beacon lit:** "The beacon still guides Yard traffic in at night. The Narrows batteries know our boats are coming."
- **If op 4 took the East Slope Comms (intel on the Yard's shifts):** "The Yard's crews change at the Chora at dusk†. Hit it on the change and half the garrison is on the Skala path†."
- **If only op 3 led here:** "We come from the west, by boat to Ash's north tip. It is the long way round, and the only way open."
