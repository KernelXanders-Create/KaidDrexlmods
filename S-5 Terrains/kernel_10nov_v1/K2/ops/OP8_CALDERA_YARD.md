# Op 8: The Caldera Yard (week 4), PROPOSED briefing

† marks something I invented that is not in the purpose map.

## Briefing
**Situation.**
- **Ground.** This is the reason the planet is held. Inside the flooded crater, the CIS build frigates in a hidden yard on Ember Crescent's inner shore: a dry-dock cradle with a frigate hull, two slipway gantries and the Rim Foundry.
- **Enemy.**
  - **Kameni Shield Generator:** on Kameni, the black lava cone in the lagoon. It blocks air strikes on the Yard until it is blown.
  - **Breach Dam:** closes the lagoon's south-west gap. Its gate opens the lagoon to boats.
  - **South arm guns** cover the lagoon and the dam.
  - **Akrotar Dig:** the CIS are stripping a buried ancient city out of the ash. The Yard's ammunition and droid store sits in the dig sheds.
- **Friendly.** Depending on what ran before, we hold the Rim Chora and the Skala path head (op 7), Causeway Foot and the south-arm road (op 4) and Sound Head (op 3).

**Mission.** Destroy the Caldera Yard and clear the Rim Foundry. This ends the run on this planet.

**Command chooses.** The op is won with the **Caldera Yard + Rim Foundry** plus **any two** of:
- **Breach Dam** (platoon): opens the lagoon to Republic boats; its crest road links the two crescents.
- **Kameni Shield Generator** (two-squad): lets air strike the Yard. Boat or air only.
- **South Arm Gun Line** (two-squad): populated from the start for air prep.
- **Yard Power Plant** (platoon): darkens the foundry's halls and also feeds the Kameni shield.

Supporting sites pay off if you take them:
- the Dry Dock Pump House strands the hull in the cradle;
- the Inner Shore Road Gate is the southern way into the Yard;
- the Akrotar Dig makes a big secondary burn;
- the south crest and Akrotar AA open the southern sky;
- the Ash Crescent sites (tower, crest guns, outer depot) open Ash's west beach and silence the guns firing across the lagoon.

**Getting there.** Any of these, and command picks:
1. Down the Skala path from the Chora (op 7).
2. Along the south-arm road from Causeway Foot (op 4).
3. By boat from Sound Head over the Pumice Shoals channel to Ash Crescent and the dam's west end (op 3).
4. By boat through the North Narrows (op 7), or through the dam gate once it opens.
5. By air, which can't strike the Yard until the Kameni shield is down.

No landing zones are given. No order is set.

**After the op.** The Yard is destroyed and the capstone cleared. The planet's run is over.

## Zeus brief (5 lines)
1. **Held:** the Yard and Rim Foundry (the planet's one capstone interior), the Breach Dam, Kameni, the south arm guns and AA, the Akrotar Dig, and the Ash Crescent guns. 18 sites, planned garrison about 990 if all filled. The capstone interior counts toward the 1,200 alive.
2. **Reserve:** for the Yard, from inside the foundry and the crater wall depot; for the south arm, from the Akrotar side; for Ash Crescent and Kameni, only their own garrisons. Spawn 250-400 m out, unseen.
3. **Kameni's shield stays up until the generator is blown.** The Yard Power Plant feeds it, so blowing the plant may also drop it if the mission wants that.
4. **The dam crest is the only AI link between the crescents,** and it is unproven in engine. If it fails, Ash keeps its own garrison. No droid boats in the lagoon.
5. **Must not happen:** no scripted artillery from the south arm or Ash crest guns; the frigate hull never launches or leaves the cradle.

## History (pick by the results before; op 8 can follow op 3, 4 or 7)
- **If op 7 was won (the Chora is ours):** "From the Chora we can see the cradle and the hull in it. The Skala path is open. Tonight† we go down."
- **If op 7 opened the North Narrows (both batteries down):** "The Narrows are open. Our boats can enter the lagoon from the north without waiting for the dam."
- **If op 4 opened the south-arm road:** "The south-arm road is clear to the dam. Armour can reach the Yard's southern gate."
- **If op 7's Crew Barracks gave the shift roster:** "We know when the Yard's shifts change. The foundry floor will be thinnest then."
- **If op 7 was not won:** "The Chora still holds the cliff. We come at the Yard from the south arm and the water, with the rim guns above us."
