# Op 5: Acropolis Isle (week 3), PROPOSED briefing

† marks something I invented that is not in the purpose map.

## Briefing
**Situation.**
- **Ground.** Acropolis Isle is a 340 m hill island in the north-west corner, 2.1 km across the Kastro Strait from Sound Isle.
- **Enemy.**
  - The CIS regional command sits in the old acropolis on its summit, watching the whole north-west sea.
  - Sci-fi windmills on the island's spine power the citadel's shields, with flak among them.
  - Boat pens hide in the sea caves under the white pumice cliffs and run the Yard's northern supply.
  - Kastro Quay, the harbour lower town, is their supply port.
  - 2.3 km east, the Lantern Rock beacon guides Yard traffic into the North Narrows.
  - On the Coral Shelf, pillboxes and a wrecked landing craft cover the boat channels toward Oyra Head.
- **Friendly.** We hold Sound Head and its boatyard.

**Mission.** Take the Acropolis Citadel. Cut the northern supply line. Take Kastro Quay as the next FOB.

**Command chooses.** The op is won with the **Acropolis Citadel** plus **any two** of:
- **Windmill Ridge** (two-squad): blowing it cuts the citadel's shields.
- **Sea Cave Pens** (platoon): stops the northern supply boats. This is one of the map's two tunnel sites.
- **Kastro Quay** (platoon): the port town and the next FOB.
- **Lantern Rock Beacon** (two-squad): blinds the Narrows to night traffic. Boat or air only.

Supporting sites pay off if you take them:
- the Ridge Power Station also drops the citadel's shields;
- the Citadel Gate Checkpoint opens the vehicle way up;
- the Olive Terraces Post opens a covered climb;
- the Pumice Coast Bunker Line opens the south beach;
- the East Bay Landers open a beach behind Kastro Quay.

Clearing the Coral Shelf sites opens the boat route to Oyra Head for op 7. The Citadel Gun Park is populated from the start for air prep. The Kastro Strait sensor stack can only be hit from the air, and killing it lets the crossing arrive unseen.

**Getting there.**
- **Boats** cross the Kastro Strait (about 3 minutes; MEASURED 2.1 km) or go north round the cape.
- **LAATs** go anywhere once the West Cliff AA is down.
- **No landing zones are given. No order is set.** Hitting the shields first makes the citadel easier; going straight for the citadel is allowed.

**After the op.** The FOB moves to Kastro Quay; if it is not taken, the FOB goes in the acropolis. Boats from Kastro Quay can reach Oyra Head through the Coral Shelf (op 7).

## Zeus brief (5 lines)
1. **Held:** the citadel, Windmill Ridge, the sea-cave pens, Kastro Quay, the island's coast and AA, plus static sites on Lantern Rock and the Coral Shelf. 19 sites, planned garrison about 930 if all filled.
2. **Reserve:** on Acropolis Isle from the citadel's north slope (the gun park side), 250-400 m out and unseen. Lantern Rock has only the beacon garrison and the hamlet picket.
3. **No droid crosses water.** The Coral Shelf pillboxes and the wreck are static garrisons.
4. **The citadel's shield drops only when the windmills or the power station are blown.** Don't drop it by script.
5. **Must not happen:** no scripted artillery from the North Cape Battery; no LZs marked; the sea-cave pens never launch droid boats at players.
