# Alien layer C: crystal veins in black lava (Islands "Ember Caldera", PROPOSED reference page)

**The idea:** the black and red volcanic rock of the caldera carries crystal: veins and geode pockets in basalt, glassy obsidian flows, green olivine sand, and hydrothermal colour fields. The purpose map already puts red crystal on Kameni and the Cinder Cone. These references show how real volcanic ground does it. Facts and looks only; no ranking.

## How it would look on Ember Caldera (design notes, ASSUMED)
- **Where:**
  - crystal veins and geode pockets in the inner caldera cliffs, the Prophet quarry and the Akrotar Dig;
  - an obsidian lobe and vent colour field on Kameni;
  - a green olivine beach under the Cinder Cone;
  - black column walls on the Black Strand.
- **Assets (REV2 list):** alien crystals and Gliese rocks are owned; black volcanic rock is missing. The crystal colour should stay red, as the purpose map says (only on Kameni and the Cinder Cone), so the planet keeps one consistent crystal colour. These references show purple, green and blue are also real, as options for Miran.
- **Engine:** crystals and rock as simple objects (04). **Distant read:** vein colour won't show past about 1.8 km, so the colour fields (black, green sand, sulfur yellow) must be in the satellite texture.

## Facts to keep straight
- Dates and sizes differ between sources: the Rocche Rosse age, and the size of the largest Naica crystal. I quote ranges.
- Blue fire is burning sulfur gas, not blue lava, even though articles often call it "blue lava".
