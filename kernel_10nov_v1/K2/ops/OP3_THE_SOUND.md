# Op 3: The Sound (week 2), PROPOSED briefing

† marks something I invented that is not in the purpose map. Everything else comes from PURPOSE_MAP.md v2 and objectives_v2.json.

## Briefing
**Situation.**
- **Ground.** Sound Isle lies 2.2 km north-west of Black Strand across the Strand Sound. It is tall and ringed by sea cliffs. Its one sheltered anchorage, the Sound, cuts into it from the east.
- **Enemy.** The CIS have sealed the Sound mouth with a sea gate between two gun keeps. Walls and keeps also plug the two gullies that break the cliffs, the South Notch and the West Notch. Sound Head, the town at the head of the inlet, is their boatyard for the west rim. Guns on Ash Crescent's outer slope and pillboxes on the Pumice Shoals cover the channel east of the island.
- **Friendly.** We hold Black Strand and the FOB in Perys. Boats and LAATs stage from Perys quay.

**Mission.** Blow the Sound Gate and open the Sound to Republic boats. Take Sound Head as the next FOB.

**Command chooses.** The op is won with the **Sound Gate** plus **any two** of these four:
- **South Notch Keep** (platoon): the main way into the island from the south-east beach.
- **West Notch Keep** (two-squad): a second way in, from the open west coast.
- **Sound Head** (platoon): the town and the next FOB.
- **Summit AA** (two-squad): opens the island's sky to LAATs.

Supporting sites pay off if you take them:
- the South Cape Landers give a second beach (and the FOB if Sound Head holds out);
- the North Cove Outpost opens a back door to Sound Head;
- the Cliff Galleries let you take the gate's north headland from behind;
- the Peak Relay Dish cuts the walls' warning to the Yard.

The Ash Crescent sites (comms and south gun line) are optional. Taking them gives the first look into the lagoon and makes the Pumice Shoals crossing safe for later ops.

**Getting there.**
- **Boats or LAATs, your choice.** No landing zones are given: platoons pick their own beaches and clearings.
- **Boat crossing.** The Strand Sound is about 3 minutes by boat (MEASURED 2.2 km; boat speed ASSUMED).
- **Air.** The Sound Rim Guns are populated from the start for pilots to soften.
- **No order is set.** Gate first, notches first, or town first all work.

**After the op.** The FOB moves to Sound Head; if Sound Head is not taken, the FOB goes to the South Cape beach. Boats can then reach Acropolis Isle (op 5) and Ash Crescent (op 8).

## Zeus brief (5 lines)
1. **Held:** the Sound fortress (the gate, two headland keeps and two notch keeps), Sound Head town, the Summit AA and Ash Crescent's outer west slope. 21 sites, planned garrison about 1,005 if all filled (MEASURED / ASSUMED). Fill on approach to stay under 1,200 alive.
2. **Reserve:** the island's own garrison only, spawning 250-400 m out, unseen and on the enemy side. Sound Isle reserves come from the inland ramp depot and the summit side. Ash Crescent has its own small garrison.
3. **No droid crosses water.** The Pumice Shoals pillboxes and the shoal wreck fort are static.
4. **Guns are targets only.** The rim guns and the west cliff battery never fire scripted artillery at players.
5. **Must not happen:** the gate never opens by script before it is blown; no LZs are marked; no droid boats ferry reinforcements.
