# Op 2: Airfield Terrace (week 1, Saturday 14 Nov, 20:00 US Eastern), PROPOSED briefing

† marks something I invented that is not in `ops12/sites_ops_1_2.md` or the purpose map.

## Briefing
**Situation.**
- **Ground.** The Airfield Terrace is a raised, flat-topped marine terrace on the south-east of the map, edged by cliffs. Wreck Cove lies under the cliffs, where a CIS freighter ran aground; a stair climbs from the cove to the terrace. The causeway north toward the caldera starts at the terrace's north end.
- **Enemy.** CIS droid fighters fly from the Super Airbase on the terrace and cover the whole caldera sky. Around it:
  - a fuel farm and a munitions store;
  - a control tower;
  - AA bases on the west and east terrace edges;
  - turbolaser guns on the south cliff, covering the southern sea;
  - a radar on Radar Point watching the fleet's southern anchorage.
- **Friendly.** The company is aboard the ship south of the Airfield Terrace. Black Strand's result from this afternoon is in the history below.

**Mission.** Take the Terrace Airbase. Ground the CIS fighters for the rest of the campaign. The airbase becomes the Republic FARP.

**Command chooses.** The op is won with the **Terrace Airbase** plus **any two** of:
- **Terrace Fuel Farm** (platoon): the fighters stay grounded without it; a big burn.
- **Control Tower** (two-squad): its logs show the frigate schedule of the yard inside the caldera.
- **West Terrace AA** (two-squad): lets LAATs land troops on the terrace from the west.
- **East Terrace AA** (two-squad): opens the causeway and the eastern approach to air cover.

Supporting sites pay off if you take them:
- **Parked Droid Fighters:** populated from the start for pilots to soften.
- **South Cliff Guns:** silencing them opens the south cliffs to boat landings.
- **Wreck Cove and the Cliff Stair Post:** a way up from the cove, plus the freighter's manifest (intel on the yard).
- **Radar Point:** blinds the airbase's early warning.
- **Terrace Power Bank:** slows the East Terrace AA.
- **Workers' Camp, North Beach Landing and West Terrace Depot:** covered start lines and a second beach.
- **Causeway Head Checkpoint:** opens next week's road north.

**Getting there.**
- **From the ship, by boat or LAAT.** No landing zones are given.
- **Approaches:**
  - the south cliffs, once their guns are quiet;
  - Wreck Cove and the stair;
  - the north beach;
  - straight onto the terrace by air, once the AA on that side is down.
- **No order is set.**

**After the op.**
- The airbase is the Republic FARP; air arrives faster for the rest of the run.
- The causeway head is open for next week's push to the Cinder Cone.
