# Op 1: Black Strand (week 1, Saturday 14 Nov, 13:00 US Eastern), PROPOSED briefing

† marks something I invented that is not in `ops12/sites_ops_1_2.md` or the purpose map. Op 1 is the opener, so it has no history lines.

## Briefing
**Situation.**
- **Ground.** Black Strand is a low shield volcano with a long black beach on its south side. Lava ribs run down across the beach. A reef of sea stacks, the Stack Garden, lies just east, joined to the strand by a reef flat you can wade.
- **Enemy.** The CIS hold the whole island. Their main defences:
  - A **beach line** on the strand: a metal trench with guns over the black beach, and a walled redoubt behind it.
  - **Gun pits** dug into a lava rib, which enfilade the beach from the west, with a walled citadel guarding their flank.
  - **Shield pylons** on the ridge, powered by a geothermal plant inland. They shield the beach guns until the plant or the pylons are blown.
  - **A flak crown** on the summit, which owns the sky over the beach. A radar on an offshore sea stack cues it onto our LAATs.
- **Perys,** the island's harbour village, sits on the hill above the north-east shore. It is the CIS depot for the island.
- **Friendly.** The company is aboard the ship lying south of Black Strand. There is no ground FOB.

**Mission.** Seize Perys and take Black Strand. Perys becomes the Republic FOB for the weeks ahead.

**Command chooses.** The op is won with **Perys** plus **any two** of these four:
- **Strand Bunker Line** (platoon): opens the south beach to boats.
- **Geothermal Plant** (platoon): drops the shields over the beach guns.
- **Lava Rib Gun Pits** (two-squad): opens the west half of the beach.
- **Summit Crater Post** (two-squad): opens the island's sky to LAATs.

Supporting sites pay off if you take them:
- **Offshore Sensor Stack:** air only. Kill it and the first landings arrive unannounced.
- **Vent Field Radio:** the first intel on what the CIS are building inside the caldera.
- **Ash Field Depot:** the gun line's ammunition store; its logs map the island's guns.
- **West Cove Landers:** a second landing beach.
- **Arch Islet Fort and the Reef Pillboxes:** make the reef wade from the Stack Garden safe.
- **Crater Road Checkpoint:** opens the ring road from Perys to the summit.
- **Mortar Terraces:** taking them stops the mortars on the Perys approach.

**Getting there.**
- **From the ship, by boat or LAAT.** No landing zones are given: command picks the beach, the cove or the reef.
- **Air prep:** the Droid Artillery Park behind the beach is populated from the start for pilots to soften. The Summit Crater flak is waiting for the first LAATs unless the sensor stack goes first.
- **No order is set.** Break the beach and climb to Perys, land in the west cove and come round, or take the sky first and go straight for the town.

**After the op.**
- If Perys is taken, it becomes the Republic FOB and its quay takes our boats.
- If not, the next ops start from the ships again.

## Zeus brief (5 lines)
1. **Held:** Perys (the final), the beach line and redoubt, the gun pits and their citadel, the geothermal plant and pylons, the summit flak, and the coast and reef sites. Size classes total about 955 if every site filled at once (purpose map). Fill on approach, Zeus included, under 1,200 alive.
2. **Reserve:** the island's own garrison only, spawning 250-400 m out, unseen, from the inland side: the ash field and the vent field for the beach, the ring road behind Perys for the town. The Stack Garden and the arch islet hold only their own garrisons.
3. **No droid crosses water:** the reef pillboxes and the arch fort are static. The sensor stack has no garrison; it is a target only.
4. **Guns and mortars fire only at Zeus's hand,** never by script at players. The Mortar Terraces' pieces are Zeus-fired targets (see the open question in the README).
5. **Must not happen:** no landing zones marked; the beach shields don't drop by script before the plant or the pylons are blown; nothing spawns inside Perys after the players are in it.
