# Alien layer B: scrub and trees in non-Earth colours (Islands "Ember Caldera", PROPOSED reference page)

**The idea:** keep the dry Aegean hillside (terraces, scattered scrub, olives near the towns) but swap the plants' shapes and colours for ones that read as "not Earth": umbrella trees, swollen trunks, red and silver plants, octopus-armed thorn trees. Facts and looks only; no ranking.

## How it would look on Ember Caldera (design notes, ASSUMED)
- **Bands:**
  - low coastal flats get a red ground layer (Panjin);
  - terraces and town edges get swollen trunks and umbrella trees (Socotra), with real olives kept only in the town gardens;
  - dry slopes get spiny thickets (Madagascar);
  - high crests get silver rosettes (Haleakalā) and red spires (Teide).
- **Assets (REV2 list):** blue and red-leaf trees, Gliese plants, alien brush and sci-fi windmills are owned. Olive, fig, oleander, palm and pine are owned for the "Greek" side. I have not checked whether any owned model has the umbrella-crown or bottle-trunk shape, so that is ASSUMED.
- **Palette:** red, silver and blue-green against white towns and a deep-blue sea. This stays clear of Sarrak's tan.
- **Engine:** trees are normal vegetation objects. Dense thickets cost FPS and AI sight checks (04), so keep the densest stands to slopes away from the objectives. **Distant read:** the red ground layer and the scrub tint must be in the satellite texture.

## Facts to keep straight
- *Dracaena cinnabari* is threatened, with poor natural regeneration (Wikipedia). It is a look reference only.
- Silversword is monocarpic: it flowers once and dies. That is a visual idea only.
