# Alien layer A: a coral shore (Islands "Ember Caldera", PROPOSED reference page)

**The idea:** keep the Aegean shapes (coves, capes, small harbours) but build the shoreline from living or raised coral in non-Earth colours: pink and red sand, red pipe-coral heads, pale coral platforms with pools. Facts and looks only; no ranking against B or C.

## How it would look on Ember Caldera (design notes, ASSUMED)
- **Where:** the Stack Garden reef, the Strand wade, the Coral Shelf and the Akrotar cape. These are already reef and shoal places in the purpose map, so the alien coral takes over the existing reef zones and adds no new landform.
- **Palette:** pink-to-coral beach bands, red coral heads, cream coral platforms, deep-blue water. It stays clear of Sarrak's tan, and the black lava beaches elsewhere give contrast.
- **Assets:** Rugosa coral (owned) as heads and outcrops; the satellite texture carries the pink sand. Black volcanic rock is listed as missing in REV2, so this layer uses coral and does not depend on it.
- **Engine:** coral heads and outcrops as simple objects (04: rocks and dressing must be simple). **Distant read:** the pink and cream bands must be in the satellite texture, because objects vanish past about 1.8 km.

## Facts to keep straight
- Pink beaches are rare on Earth. Travel sources often say "one of about seven", but that count isn't verified.
- The Komodo sources differ on whether coral or foraminifera matters more.
